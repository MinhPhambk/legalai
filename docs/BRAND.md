# LegalAI – brand guide (summary)

The full, interactive guideline lives at **`/brand`** (and `/en/brand`) in the web app. It is public but `noindex`. This file is the short version plus the asset list.

## Positioning
- **Name:** LegalAI (unchanged).
- **Tagline:** "Trợ lý pháp lý AI cho doanh nghiệp Việt" / "AI legal assistant for Vietnamese businesses".
- **Endorsement:** "Sản phẩm của FTU Tech Lab – Trường Đại học Ngoại thương" / "A product of FTU Tech Lab, Foreign Trade University".
- **Status:** research pilot. Always say "not legal advice". Never state stats, customers, testimonials, awards, partner logos or prices.

## Logo
- **Mark (square icon):** the official **TECHLAB** logo of FTU Tech Lab (`client/public/brand/techlab-logo.png`, taken unchanged from the Lab's deck, 377×152) centred on a square tile of its own red `#B71B21` (6/32 corner radius), so logo and tile read as one block.
  - Used for favicon, PWA / app icons, the assistant avatar and the sidebar.
  - Below ~48 px the tagline is not legible; that is accepted (the red block + "TECHLAB" stays recognisable).
- **Lockups:** TECHLAB logo + "LegalAI" wordmark (Be Vietnam Pro Bold, outlined), horizontal and stacked, for light (`color`) and dark (`color-on-dark`) backgrounds. Used in the site header / footer and the Open Graph images.
- **No recoloured variants:** the TECHLAB file is an official raster – never recolour, crop, stretch, rotate or add effects.
- The TECHLAB file is small: do not scale it above ~150 px tall; ask the Lab for an SVG / high-resolution original before print use.
- **Clear space:** at least ¼ of the mark height on every side. **Minimum size:** mark 16 px; horizontal lockup 96 px wide.
- **The Foreign Trade University logo** appears only in the endorsement area:
  - Use the official file unchanged (`client/public/brand/ftu-logo.png`).
  - On dark backgrounds, place it on a white rounded plate.
  - Keep it at least ~200 px wide, because the file contains small text.

## Voice & tone (VI / EN)
Clear, honest, grounded and respectful:
- Use short sentences.
- State limitations and how certain the answer is.
- Every legal claim needs a source.
- Never be alarmist.

## To fill in before launch
- **`client/src/site/config.js` → `CONTACT_EMAIL`:** currently the placeholder `lienhe@example.com`. It is used by every "Request access" link.
- **`SITE_URL` environment variable:** the real domain, e.g. `https://legalai.example.vn`. It drives the canonical, hreflang, OG and sitemap URLs. It also turns on indexing, but only for that host and never for `*.trycloudflare.com`.
- **Privacy policy and terms (`/privacy`, `/terms`):** the texts are drafts marked "Bản nháp / Draft" and need legal review.
